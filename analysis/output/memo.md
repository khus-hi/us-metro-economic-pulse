# US Metro Economic Pulse: September 2026

*Top 50 US metros. Data through September 2026 (BLS); analyst pay OEWS May 2025; cost of living BEA RPP 2024. For economic development offices and analyst job seekers.*

## Headline
- **Hiring is cooling.** The median metro added 0.46% more jobs than a year earlier, vs 0.55% 3 months ago.
- **Unemployment is higher than a year ago in 16 of 50 metros** and lower in 30.
- **Real wages:** median growth +0.08 pp after local inflation; real hourly earnings fell in 24 of 50 metros.

## Where momentum is
- **Strongest pulse:** Salt Lake City (+1.16), Raleigh (+0.91), Charlotte (+0.84).
- **Weakest pulse:** Richmond, VA (-1.13), Indianapolis (-1.04), Denver (-0.96).
- **Gaining fastest (3 months):** Portland (+0.76), Miami (+0.68), Houston (+0.57).
- **Losing ground fastest:** Memphis, TN (-0.61), Denver (-0.57), Richmond, VA (-0.52).

## Boston vs peers
- Pulse -0.37, #38 of 50 and #6 of 7 among peers (New York, Philadelphia, Washington, San Francisco, San Jose, Seattle).
- Job growth 0.08% YoY; unemployment 3.9% (-0.3 pp YoY); real wage growth -0.66 pp.

## For analyst job seekers
- **Data Scientists pay after cost of living:** San Jose $167,610 ($185,080 nominal); Seattle $148,237 ($164,740 nominal); San Francisco $147,137 ($170,110 nominal).
- Boston: $132,040 median, $121,959 after cost of living (#14 of 50).
- **Open analyst postings:** 374 across 38 companies' job boards, 169 of them in a top-50 metro. Most: New York (99), San Francisco (76), Seattle (26) (a posting listed in several metros counts in each).
- **Most-requested tools:** SQL 63%, Python 45%, Statistics 32%, Excel 26% of postings.

## Watchlist: unemployment nowcast for October 2026
- Largest expected year-over-year rises: Hartford 5.1% (+0.9 pp), Minneapolis 4.6% (+0.8 pp), Detroit 5.7% (+0.7 pp).
- Backtest accuracy (MAPE): model 4.87%, seasonal naive 4.92%, last value 7.20%. Treat as a watchlist, not a forecast of record.

## Data notes
- Pulse = weighted z-scores of job growth, unemployment change, real wage growth and openings, relative to the other 49 metros (0 = average).
- Openings are state-level JOLTS, last published for December 2025; metros in one state share it.
- 29 metros use Census-division CPI (BLS publishes metro CPI for 21 of the 50).
- BLS published no October 2025 metro data (federal shutdown).
- Postings come from public Greenhouse/Lever boards of the companies in seeds/companies.csv, not the whole market.
